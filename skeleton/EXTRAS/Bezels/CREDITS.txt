Screen bezels

Shown around games when "Screen Bezel" is on (in a game's menu: Options > Frontend).
A game uses Bezels/<TAG>.png (eg. GBC.png), or Bezels/<TAG>/<game file name>.png for that game alone.
Extras/ holds other Game Boy looks (GB.png is the Pocket one): copy one over GB.png to use it.

All bezels except VB.png are by drkhrse, from their Miyoo bezel pack:
https://github.com/drkhrse/drkhrse_miyoo_bezels
Thank you, drkhrse!

VB.png (Virtual Boy) was made for MinUI+ in the same style.

The arcade bezels (CPS1, CPS2, CPS3, FBN, MAME) were made for MinUI+ in the same style, in MinUI's font
(tools/borders/make-arcade-borders.py). Arcade games fill the screen, so they only show beside vertical games
played Upright: the bands move in to meet the game, whatever its shape.

The <TAG>-native.png bezels (GB, GBC, GBA, MGBA, GG, NGP, NGPC) are used at native scaling, where the game is
smaller than the normal bezels expect: each handheld's face in its shell color, made for MinUI+
(tools/borders/make-native-borders.py) around drkhrse's logos and power lights. Any system can have one:
Bezels/<TAG>-native.png, with its see-through window where the game is at native scaling.

GBA.png and MGBA.png carry a holographic GAME BOY ADVANCE logo in their bottom bar (tools/borders/make-gba-logo.py,
from tools/borders/gba-logo.png); drkhrse's original is Extras/GBA-classic.png. The Game Boy Color native bezel's
logo is drawn from tools/borders/gbc-logo.png. Game Boy, Game Boy Color and Game Boy Advance are Nintendo's trademarks.
